\title{ScrollFiesta: A Virtual Meshing and Unwrapping Toolkit for the Herculaneum Papyri}

\renewcommand{\shortauthors}{ScrollFiesta}

\begin{abstract}
Virtually unwrapping a carbonized Herculaneum papyrus poses a geometry problem of an unusual shape. The target is not the boundary of a solid but a single open sheet, many meters long, wound into a hundred or more turns that touch, fuse, delaminate, and tear. Standard machinery fits it badly: isosurfacing and Poisson reconstruction recover closed, two-sided boundaries; a flattener minimizes distortion against whatever mesh it is handed, so two fused turns flatten into one clean page; and the freedom an atlas packer relies on --- place a chart wherever space allows --- is exactly the freedom this problem lacks, because horizontal position on the page \emph{is} developed arc length, shared by every fragment of a turn and fixed only up to a whole number of turns.

We present \emph{ScrollFiesta}, a meshing and unwrapping toolkit organized around one geometric instrument: a branch-cut-free winding coordinate that measures, for any two samples, whether they lie on the same turn of the roll or on different ones. It reappears as the decision variable at every scale, and it is the quantity no mesh-quality measure can see.

ScrollFiesta takes a completed surface-probability volume as its input; producing that volume is the subject of the companion \emph{Socratic Method} report, and is deliberately out of scope here. We contribute three pieces. \emph{First}, we separate local geometry from large-scale identity. Geometry is extracted per cube by an iterated moving-least-squares collapse, ball pivoting guarded by anti-parallel and accumulated-winding tests, topology repair, and CVT remeshing; identity is carried independently as sheet tracks whose pairwise integer winding relations compose through a maximum-support forest with integer subtree-shift loop closure, so geometry is never fused to make an order graph connected. \emph{Second}, winding is inferred rather than obtained by rounding one fragile measurement. A two-sided generalized winding number field supplies noisy absolute per-component observations, local continuation and radial-order measurements supply pairwise factors, and an integer-label Markov random field reconciles both by alpha--beta swap graph cuts. \emph{Third}, the accepted geometry is fitted to a ribbon of quadrilaterals whose horizontal coordinate is exact developed arc length, reconciled by Huber-IRLS sparse least squares, untangled by exact triangle-contact response, and composited against CT.

On a fresh $4\times5\times5$ block the winding MRF resolves 882,219 vertices in 10.8 seconds, lowering energy from 221.37 to 200.49 at continuation/order satisfaction 0.9997/0.9982; sheet books carry over 70\% of evidenced material at every rung through $21\times21\times21$, ordering 15,457 tracks across 112 turns; and the metric fit conserves all 4.03M faces of the $4\times5\times5$ ribbon in 9.6 seconds. We also report the negative results that shaped the design, including a slab splitter that separates 0 of 246 fused runs and an overlap-minimizing tabu atlas that removes 99.5\% of UV overlaps while tearing 41 weld relations --- both instances of a geometric objective being asked to decide a question geometry cannot see.
\end{abstract}
